# Data provenance

The active catalog is generated by the authored `data/` module.
[Source attribution](../../data/NOTICE.md) applies to these values.
[release.json](release.json) records the complete input lock, and
[semantic_sources.json](semantic_sources.json) records the independent semantic
snapshot versions and counts. Source materials remain subject to their
respective terms. Raw packs and executables are not included.
